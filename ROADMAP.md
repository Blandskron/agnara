# Roadmap

Maintain one capability across direct Python, HTTP and MCP with executable
evidence from installed packages. Continue reference application and
interoperability work. Evaluate new hosts, persistence and protocol
projections through their contracts, lifecycle ownership and feasibility
tests.

The [backlog](BACKLOG.md) owns pending work. The
[`docs/MATURITY.md`](docs/MATURITY.md) matrix owns current support and evidence.

Per maintainer direction on 2026-09-28, package publication remains paused
until approximately March 2027. Work continues on `develop` under
`[Unreleased]`. Publication requires a separate maintainer decision and the
[release gates](docs/MAINTAINERS_RELEASE.md).
