# Agnara Code of Conduct

## A welcoming community

Agnara welcomes people with different backgrounds, abilities, identities and
levels of experience. Participation should be respectful and free from
harassment, whether someone is asking a question, contributing code or
reviewing a decision. These expectations apply to maintainers as well as
contributors.

## How we work together

- Discuss ideas and evidence without attacking the person presenting them.
- Make room for questions, accessibility needs and different communication styles.
- Give constructive feedback, accept corrections and acknowledge mistakes.
- Respect people's time, privacy, boundaries and decisions to leave a conversation.
- Keep disagreement focused on the project and follow moderation instructions.

Using an AI assistant does not remove responsibility for submitted content or
interactions. The same standards apply to comments and contributions produced
with automation.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment or unwanted sexual
attention are not acceptable. Neither are personal insults, intimidation,
publishing someone else's private information without permission, retaliation
against a reporter, or repeated disruption after a moderator asks it to stop.
Good-faith technical disagreement and criticism of project decisions are
welcome; they do not excuse abusive behavior.

## Scope

This policy covers the repository's Issues, Pull Requests, reviews and comments,
and other spaces explicitly operated by Agnara. It also covers interactions
where someone explicitly represents the project. It does not give the
maintainer authority over unrelated private conversations or communities.

## Report a concern privately

Email [contacto@blandskron.com](mailto:contacto@blandskron.com), monitored by
the project maintainer, Blandskron. Include what happened, relevant links or
dates, and how you would prefer to be contacted. Share only the personal
information needed to understand the concern. You may report an incident you
witnessed; you do not have to confront the person involved first.

Avoid posting sensitive reports in public Issues. Reports will be handled
discreetly, with information shared only as needed to investigate and respond.
Absolute confidentiality cannot be promised: investigating an incident may
require discussing relevant facts with those involved. The maintainer will
seek to avoid exposing the reporter unnecessarily.

If the report concerns the maintainer or you are uncomfortable using that
channel, GitHub's [abuse reporting process](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
is an independent route for behavior that violates GitHub's rules. This
project currently has no separate conduct review committee.

Security vulnerabilities have a different private channel described in the
[security policy](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Blandskron is responsible for reviewing reports and enforcing this policy in
project spaces. Decisions should consider the impact, available evidence,
severity and any repeated behavior. The maintainer may remove or edit abusive
content, request a correction, issue a warning, restrict participation
temporarily or exclude someone from project spaces. Serious incidents may
require immediate restrictions without an earlier warning.

Where practicable, the affected participant will receive an explanation and
can provide relevant context. Requests to reconsider a decision can be sent to
the same email with additional evidence; this is not an independent appeal.
Reporters will receive follow-up where possible without disclosing another
person's private information. No fixed response time is promised.
